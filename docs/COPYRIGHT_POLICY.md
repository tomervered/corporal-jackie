# Copyright policy

Corporal Jackie is a media player. It does not provide, host or sell any movies or shows. Add-ons and torrents come from third parties, and finding a video through them does not mean it is licensed. Play only what you have the right to watch, and share only what you have the right to share.

## What the app does and does not do

- It plays files on your computer, web links you open, and torrents you open.
- It ships with no add-on that lists streams. Cinemeta, which you can allow on the first-run screen, gives titles, posters and descriptions, not streams.
- This project keeps no list of add-ons or stream sources, recommends none, and installs none from links.
- It does not remove or get around DRM or any other copy protection.

## Choices you make

Each of these starts off. You can change it in Settings (key P).

- **Play torrents.** Off: torrents do not play, and the torrent engine never starts.
- **Upload to other peers.** Off: the app sends no part of any video to anyone. On: while a video plays, the app shares pieces of it with others in the torrent, and stops when the video closes. Sharing a video is a separate right from watching it.
- **Each add-on.** Before the app contacts a new add-on, it shows the add-on's address and asks you to confirm.
- **Each search for streams.** Opening a movie asks the add-ons nothing. They are asked when you press Find streams, or when you pick an episode.

## Your responsibility

You decide which add-ons to install and what to play. Whether a video is licensed depends on where it came from and where you live, and the app cannot check that. Downloading an unlicensed copy can infringe copyright even with uploading off.
